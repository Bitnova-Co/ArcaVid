# ArcaVid

## The AI Operating System for Social Media Creators

🚧 **Status:** Early Development (MVP in Progress)

ArcaVid is an AI-powered platform for content creation, workflow automation, and channel analytics. It helps creators, businesses, and teams create content, automate repetitive tasks, and gain actionable insights from their social media presence—all from a unified workspace.

---

## What is ArcaVid?

ArcaVid is more than an AI writing tool.

It is a unified platform designed to simplify the entire content lifecycle—from ideation and creation to publishing, analytics, and workflow automation.

Whether you're a solo creator, growing brand, or agency, ArcaVid aims to reduce repetitive work and help you create and manage content more efficiently.

---

## Core Capabilities

### SparkChat — Your Personal ArcaVid Workspace Assistant

SparkChat is the central conversational interface of ArcaVid. It acts as your personal workspace assistant, helping you create content, access platform capabilities, and orchestrate workflows through natural language.

Features include:

* Unified conversational interface across ArcaVid
* Context-aware assistance and guidance
* Natural-language access to platform capabilities
* Content planning and workflow support
* Integration with slash commands and automations

---

### Slash Commands

ArcaVid provides a growing collection of AI-powered slash commands that allow creators to quickly access platform capabilities from a single interface.

Examples include:

* `/script`
* `/ideas`
* `/research`
* `/image`
* `/video`
* `/prompt`
* `/flow`
* `/analytics`

ArcaVid is under active development. Commands and capabilities may be introduced, refined, replaced, or removed as the platform evolves.

---

### ArcaFlow — Visual Automation Builder

Create and manage workflows without coding.

Features include:

* Visual drag-and-drop workflow builder
* Workflow generation and assistance powered by SparkChat
* Pre-built workflow templates
* Multi-step automations
* Cross-platform publishing pipelines

Example workflow:

`YouTube Video → Generate Shorts → Create Captions → Cross-post to Instagram and X`
